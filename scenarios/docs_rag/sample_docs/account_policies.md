# Account Policies

## Account Types

- **Basic Checking** — no minimum balance, $0 monthly fee, debit card included.
- **Premium Checking** — $2,500 minimum balance, waives all ATM fees worldwide, includes
  a dedicated relationship manager.
- **Savings Plus** — tiered interest rates starting at 0.5% APY, rising to 1.2% APY for
  balances over $25,000.

## Deposits and Withdrawals

- Same-day deposits are available for checks submitted before 6:00 PM local time at a
  branch, or before 8:00 PM through the mobile app.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit is $1,000 for Basic Checking and $2,500 for Premium Checking.
- Wire transfers over $10,000 require secondary verification (a one-time code sent to the
  account holder's registered phone number).

## Overdraft Policy

- The bank does not charge overdraft fees on transactions under $5.
- Standard overdraft fee is $25 per transaction, capped at 3 fees per day.
- Customers can opt out of overdraft coverage entirely at any time via the mobile app,
  in which case declined transactions incur no fee.

## Account Closure

- Accounts with a zero balance and no activity for 24 consecutive months are
  automatically flagged as dormant and moved to a non-interest-bearing status.
- Customers can close an account at any time with no penalty, provided all pending
  transactions have settled.
