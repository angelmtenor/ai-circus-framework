# Customer FAQ

**Q: How do I dispute a transaction?**
A: Open the mobile app, select the transaction, and tap "Dispute". Disputes are
reviewed within 5 business days. Provisional credit is issued within 24 hours for
disputes over $50 while the investigation is ongoing.

**Q: What should I do if my card is lost or stolen?**
A: Freeze the card instantly from the mobile app's "Card Controls" screen, or call the
24/7 support line. A replacement card arrives within 2-3 business days, or the same day
if requested at a branch.

**Q: How do I increase my daily ATM withdrawal limit?**
A: Temporary limit increases (up to 7 days) can be requested in-app for verified
accounts in good standing. Permanent increases require a branch visit with ID.

**Q: Can I open a joint account?**
A: Yes. Joint accounts require both account holders to be present (in person or via
video verification) during account opening, and either holder can act independently on
the account unless "dual signature required" is selected.

**Q: How is customer churn risk calculated?**
A: The bank's internal churn model looks at account tenure, product usage, activity
level, and account balance trends to flag customers who may be at risk of leaving,
so relationship managers can proactively reach out with retention offers.

**Q: What are the bank's customer service hours?**
A: Phone support is available 24/7. Branch hours are Monday-Friday 9 AM-5 PM, and
Saturday 9 AM-1 PM. In-app chat support is available 24/7 with average response times
under 2 minutes.
