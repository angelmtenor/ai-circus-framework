# Minor construction permit

Request type ID: `minor_construction_permit`

Use this for small-scale construction that still requires a permit: a backyard
shed, a fence, a deck, or similar low-impact work. Not for major renovations or new
buildings, which require a separate full building-permit process (not covered by
this desk).

## Required information

- Full name, ID type and number, phone, email.
- No separate address/location field is required — the property address and scope
  of work should be included in the description (dimensions, structure type,
  property address).

## Routing and turnaround

- Routed to: **Building & Permits Office**.
- Typical turnaround: 10 business days for review.
- The applicant may be contacted for additional plans/documentation before approval.
