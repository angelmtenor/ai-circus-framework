# Address registration

Request type ID: `address_registration`

Use this when a resident wants to register a new address (moving into the
jurisdiction, or updating their address on file) for local records, voting rolls,
and service eligibility.

## Required information

- Full name, ID type and number, phone, email.
- No additional address/location field is required beyond the applicant's own
  registration — the resident's stated address is captured in the description.

## Routing and turnaround

- Routed to: **Civil Registry Office**.
- Typical turnaround: 5 business days.
- The applicant is notified by email once the registration is confirmed.
