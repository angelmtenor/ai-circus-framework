# bank_early_warning sample data

`bank_early_warning.csv` — 8,196 US FDIC-insured banks, one row each, generated by
`scripts/prepare_bank_early_warning_dataset.py`.

**Source:** the FDIC BankFind Suite API (<https://api.fdic.gov/banks/docs/>) —
`financials` (call reports at 2007-12-31 and 2008-12-31), `institutions` (name, city,
state, headquarters coordinates, charter class, establishment date) and `failures`
(banks closed 2009-01-01 .. 2010-12-31). Data produced by the US federal government is
in the **public domain**; the FDIC is credited as the source. Not endorsed by the FDIC.

What the script does:

- takes each bank's 31 Dec 2008 call-report ratios as published (%), derives a few
  shares from dollar amounts (OREO, 30-89 days past due, construction and CRE loans as
  % of assets; brokered deposits as % of deposits; asset growth over 2008; age);
- clips heavy-tailed ratios to a plausible range (a tiny bank with a near-zero
  denominator can publish ratios in the thousands) and treats a missing reserve
  coverage (no noncurrent loans) as fully covered (500%);
- labels `failed_24m = 1` for the 304 banks the FDIC closed as failures in 2009-2010
  (one of the 305 failures in the window had no 2008 report); mergers and voluntary
  closures stay 0;
- drops 119 banks with missing ratios or coordinates.

`charter_type` is the institution's *last* charter on record, which for a few banks
differs from its 2008 charter.
