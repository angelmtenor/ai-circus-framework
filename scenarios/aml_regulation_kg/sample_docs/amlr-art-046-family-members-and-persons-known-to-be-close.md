# AMLR Article 46 — Family members and persons known to be close associates of politically exposed persons

_Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 31 May 2024 (AMLR), OJ L, 2024/1624, 19.6.2024. Source: EUR-Lex — © European Union, reuse authorised (Decision 2011/833/EU)._

The measures referred to in Articles 42, 44 and 45 shall also apply to family members or persons known to be close associates of politically exposed persons.
